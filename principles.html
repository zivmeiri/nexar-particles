Nexar Data Particles - Usage Principles

The Nexar Particles

Usage Principles

Principle 01

Form - The Unshaped Shape

The particle cloud has no fixed silhouette. This is intentional and non-negotiable.

The particles are always either in a state of chaos - dispersing, exploding, raw, unresolved - or in a state of emergence - coalescing into something that tells a story. A trajectory. A behavior. A truth taking shape.

There is no middle ground. A static, symmetrical, decorative blob is not a Nexar Particle. The form must always imply motion, either outward or inward. Either breaking apart or coming together. The tension between these two states is where the meaning lives.

Ask before using: Is this cloud going somewhere? Is it coming from somewhere? If the answer is neither, reshape it.

Principle 02

Scale - Infinite by Nature

The particle field never has a visible edge. Whatever frame contains it, the particles extend beyond it, cut off by the boundary, not contained by it. The viewer should always feel they are looking at a portion of something vastly larger. A window into the latent space, not the latent space itself.

No padding around the cloud. No breathing room between the particles and the frame edge. Let it escape.

This applies to all formats, slides, web, print, video. The latent space is infinite. The frame is just where we chose to look.

Principle 03

Scientific Grounding

The particles must always feel like data, not art. The difference is precision.

  • Depth of field is always present, foreground sharp, background dissolving. This creates the sense of a real three-dimensional space being observed, not illustrated.
  • The grid is always beneath. Even when barely visible. Even when it is a single faint line at the horizon. It anchors the cloud in a coordinate system.
  • Density is never uniform. Real data clusters. Real data has voids. Force both into every composition.
  • Occasional data artifacts are welcome, faint axis labels, coordinate markers, readout values in the background. Not readable. Just present. The suggestion of measurement.

The particles should feel like something a scientist would recognize, even if they could not name it.

The Standalone Elements - Icons and Marks

Principle 06

Emotional Register

The particles operate in three emotional modes. Every usage should consciously choose one:

Vast

The full cloud, dispersed, filling the frame, overwhelming. Used for scale moments. The 10 billion miles. The 45 petabytes. The full latent space.

Precise

Sparse, isolated, a single cluster examined. Used for product moments. One incident. One insight. One truth surfaced.

Alive

Particles in active transition, forming or dissolving. Used for VERA moments. Intelligence at work. The moment before the answer.
